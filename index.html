FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Do the mocks follow the latest PA syllabus?

Yes — both mock exams are prepared to follow the most recent syllabus, so what you practise matches what you'll be tested on.

What exactly is in the grading and feedback?

A skill-by-skill breakdown of your performance and written feedback on where marks were gained and lost, ending with the specific areas you should focus on next.

Should I take one mock or both?

One mock gives you a clear diagnosis. Both lets you test whether your fixes worked — Mock 1 to find the gaps, Mock 2 to confirm they're closed. Most candidates who are serious about passing take both.

What is the review session?

An optional live walkthrough of your graded mock for USD 50. We go through your answers, how marks were awarded, and build a focused plan for the time you have left.

When should I take the mocks?

Mock 1 works best once you've covered the syllabus once, leaving time to act on the feedback. Mock 2 is ideal as a final rehearsal in the weeks before your sitting.

Are you affiliated with the SOA?

No. These are independent practice mocks created by me, and are not an official SOA product or score report.
